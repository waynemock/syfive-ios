# Pentara — APP_ICON.md

Pentara’s icon should feel like it came from the same “calm, premium, modern” design lineage as Sydoku — not a loud casino app. Think *cozy evening tabletop*, lamp-on, quiet, intentional. Minimal composition, high readability at small sizes, and a refined midnight palette.

---

## Core Concept

**A single premium die** on a deep midnight background.

- One die only (no extra dice, cups, trays, tables, or props).
- The die face should show **five** (balanced, instantly recognizable, visually pleasing).
- The die should feel **modern and slightly stylized**, not photorealistic, not cartoony.
- The icon must be readable at tiny sizes: strong silhouette, clean pips, no visual clutter.

---

## Palette

### Primary “Midnight” Die Color
Use Sydoku’s midnight-ish accent color as the die’s base hue:

- `Color(red: 0.60, green: 0.50, blue: 0.90)`  
  (muted lavender / soft purple, premium—not neon)

### Background
Deep midnight indigo/blue-black with a subtle vignette:

- Suggested range: `#05060B` to `#0B0B16`
- **Predominantly dark**
- No visible texture, grain, or noise

### Pips (dots)
Warm soft white for clarity and contrast:

- Suggested: `#F3F1F8` (slightly warm)
- Optional: a *very subtle* lavender tint is acceptable, but pips must remain high-contrast.

---

## Composition & Framing

### Scale
The die should **dominate the canvas**:

- Die occupies **80–90%** of the icon height.
- Slight edge proximity is encouraged for immersion (but keep it clearly readable).
- **No large empty padding**.

### Position
- Centered composition with slight “visual bias” toward center.
- The die can be straight-on or with a slight perspective tilt (recommended), but do not overdo it.

### Lighting
- Soft, cinematic light source from **top-left**.
- Gentle highlights; no harsh specular streaks.
- Soft shadow under the die (grounding) and subtle ambient occlusion.

---

## Form & Style

### Die shape
- Rounded corners (premium).
- Subtle bevels on edges (tiny, tasteful).
- Slight gradient across the die face for depth (no obvious banding).

### Pips
- Clean circles, evenly spaced.
- Subtle micro-shadow/indentation is okay, but keep them crisp.
- Avoid glossy “bubbly” pips or glitter effects.

### Overall Aesthetic
**Calm. Premium. Modern. Minimal.**

Pentara is *not* Vegas. It’s *night table*.

---

## Strict Requirements (Non-negotiable)

- **Full-bleed 1024×1024 px** square icon.
- Background must fill the entire canvas **edge-to-edge**.
- **No white or light background anywhere**.
- **No transparency**.
- No borders, frames, rounded icon mockups, device mockups, UI, or text.
- No extra decorative elements (stars, sparkles, patterns, confetti, etc.).
- No wood texture, felt table, casino vibe, or dice cup.

---

## “Do Not Include” List

- Multiple dice
- Dice cup, tray, tabletop surface, hands, people
- Casino imagery, chips, cards, money
- Text/logos/letters
- Heavy texture/noise/grain
- Bright neon glow effects
- Gritty realism or parody

---

## DALL·E-forward Prompt (Production)

Use this prompt to generate final App Store icon artwork:

**PROMPT:**

Create a full-bleed 1024×1024 square App Store icon.

Background: a deep midnight indigo / blue-black (very dark, in the #05060B–#0B0B16 range) that fills the entire canvas edge-to-edge. No white. No transparency. No borders. No frames. No device mockups.

Main subject: a single premium die, centered, large, and dominant (occupying about 80–90% of the icon height). The die is slightly stylized and modern (not photorealistic, not cartoon), with rounded corners and a subtle bevel. Show the face with five pips clearly visible.

Color: the die’s base color is a muted lavender-purple matching Color(red: 0.60, green: 0.50, blue: 0.90). Use soft gradients for dimensionality. Pips are warm soft white (#F3F1F8) with clean circular shapes and even spacing.

Lighting: soft cinematic top-left light, gentle highlights, subtle ambient occlusion, and a soft shadow beneath the die to ground it. Add a very subtle vignette darkening toward corners. No texture overlays, no grain, no noise.

Keep the composition minimal, calm, premium, and highly readable at small sizes. No extra objects, no decorative graphics, no text.

This is final production App Store icon artwork, not a preview or mockup.

## Optional Variants (If Needed)

If you want quick alternate directions without changing the core identity:

1) **More Iconic / Minimal**
- Less perspective, flatter shading, sharper silhouette.

2) **More Cozy / Cinematic**
- Slightly warmer highlights, slightly stronger shadow, still no tabletop textures.

3) **More “Midnight Glow”**
- Very faint purple halo behind the die (extremely subtle), still predominantly dark.

(Keep each variant minimal and avoid drifting into casino/cartoony territory.)
